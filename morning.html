Rules that don't move
Light has the only hard deadline — everything else can slide. Overcast counts: cloud still delivers far more than any indoor room.
HRV before breathing, always — slow breathing raises HRV within minutes through respiratory sinus arrhythmia. Sigh first and the reading is not comparable to yesterday's.
Sighing stays out of mushin — it has a target pattern and a measurable output, which makes it an optimisation. Mushin has no target and nothing to score. Merge them and mushin becomes something you can do well or badly.
Hair after the outdoor sit — thirteen minutes outside is exactly what would undo it.
Cold never follows resistance work — training moved to after work, so the shower moved with it. Warm, at night, finishing 21:00–22:00.
Caffeine runs on a cutoff, not a delay — last dose near 15:00 against a 23:00 bedtime.
The buffer is part of the protocol — a stack with no slack fails the first morning anything goes wrong, and then it fails for good.
Why there is no walk
The walk was carrying about ninety percent light and ten percent movement. The movement is covered twice over now — evening training and the back bridge — so the only thing needing replacement was the light, and light does not require walking. Sitting outside delivers the same dose. Nothing was substituted in, because anything intense enough to be worth adding brings sweat, and sweat brings back the morning shower this design removed.
Deliberately out
Cold exposure — the weakest standalone outcome data in the stack, and it now conflicts with evening lifting.
Gratitude journalling — effects shrink as designs tighten, and it would cost mushin's slot.
Gargling for vagal tone — humming does raise nasal nitric oxide roughly fifteenfold, but the jump from there to vagal tone is invented and no health outcome was measured.
And no claim that willpower is highest early — ego depletion failed a large multi-lab replication. The real early constraint is sleep inertia: thirty to sixty minutes of genuine cognitive impairment after waking. That is why the brief sits at 07:31 and not at 07:00.
The expiry date on this version
This design works because a 07:00 wake in September puts you outdoors after sunrise. Once the clocks change and sunrise drifts toward eight, a 07:18 sit is a sit in the dark and the light step has nothing to stand on. The light box conversation comes back in November. Revisit then, not before.
The one thing
If everything else falls over: get outside by 07:18. It is the only step with a deadline, the only dose you cannot get indoors, and the one the whole day's timing hangs off.
