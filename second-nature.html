The Second Nature Protocol

Skill stops being assembled and starts being retrieved. The block is not too few reps — it is being watched while you take them. Boyd called the endpoint Fingerspitzengefühl: the feel at the fingertips that acts before deliberation gets a turn.

Change
1Stable StructureA
Automaticity is retrieval of accumulated instances of the same thing. A target that keeps changing has no instances to accumulate.
Freeze the thing being practised — for a fixed span, decided in advance. Not tuned, not improved, not extended. Bugs only.
Count reps against one shape — ten sessions of one routine beat forty across four. Redesign resets the count to zero.
Building and automatising are the opposing pair — both are needed, and you have been running one side at full for months. The freeze is how you find out what the other side does.
Where fluency already exists for you — driving, Krav — the rules were fixed and not yours to redesign. That is not a coincidence, it is the mechanism.
2Attention OutwardB
Monitoring an action while performing it keeps it in the explicit system, where it has to be rebuilt from parts every time.
One channel — during execution, attention goes to one outward thing: their face, the road, the bag, the room. Not to how you are doing.
“Not relevant” — when the optimising thought arrives, name it and return. It exists; it has no authority here.
Never review while it is live — the more the moment matters, the more true this is. That is not an exception to the rule, it is where the rule earns its keep.
This is the pillar you already built. LIVE OR OVER and “not relevant” are it. What was missing was the reason: monitoring does not just degrade the performance, it prevents the skill from ever becoming automatic.
3Bounded ReviewB
Evaluation is necessary and must have a closing edge. An assessment with no end time does not stay in the past — it leaks forward and becomes the narrative.
Fixed window — ten minutes, once, at the debrief. When it closes, the matter is finished whether or not you reached a verdict.
“Done when”, applied to yourself — the same rule that keeps you from polishing a task past its line. Quality judgements are where the fiddling lives.
Same item, fourth time — that is not analysis, that is the window failing to close. Park it and move.
4Learnable GroundA
Intuition only forms where the environment is regular enough to be learnable and gives feedback fast enough to learn from. Where either is missing, more reps produce confidence and not skill.
Run the test first — is there a pattern here that repeats, and do I find out whether I was right? Two yeses: practise. Any no: fix the environment, not yourself.
Supply the missing channel — you cannot see your own face, so expressiveness has no feedback loop and will not improve by introspection. Video supplies it. Same logic anywhere else the signal is invisible to you.
Accept the unlearnable — some domains give no reliable signal. Confidence there is a warning, not a result.
This is why driving is sharp and expressiveness is not. Nothing about you differs between the two; the feedback does.
5Structural ConstraintC
Drift runs on feedback; intention runs on values. Values lose. The protection has to be built, not resolved upon.
Close the exemption — the three-tier rule already says anything opening a new loop waits for Sunday. A new build is a new loop. That has been the one exception, and it is where the drift lives.
Building returns fast, consolidation returns slow — a card appears tonight; automaticity shows up in weeks and its signature is the absence of a signal. A feedback-driven person picks the fast one every time. That is not weakness, it is arithmetic.
Subtraction is the untried move — sixteen projects went through the first triage and none were ended. Every previous answer to this problem has been an addition.
The Freeze
Eight weeks. No new cards, no threshold tuning, no protocol adoption. Bug fixes only.
It is both the intervention and the test. If capacity rises while the building arm is off, the opposition is real and you have found which side you were over-running. If nothing changes, that is a genuine result and worth more than another card.

September is itself a load. The eight weeks are for consolidating what already exists, not for stacking a practice on top of a new job — which is the other reason the ladder starts at home.

Expect week three. That is when one small, obviously useful build will feel entirely reasonable. It will have a good argument attached. The argument is the drift, on schedule.
The Ladder
Eight rungs, rising in stakes rather than duration. It starts at home on purpose — a stable, repeating setting is what lets instances accumulate, and an unfamiliar one recruits exactly the self-monitoring this is meant to remove. One rung at a time. Do not climb until the current one is boring.
Home ground
1One household task3 min · daily
Washing up, folding, making the coffee. Attention on the physical thing — the water temperature, the weight of it, the sound. One channel, not three.
Failure signal: you are planning the next task while doing this one. The hands are here and you are not.

This is a rep, so it is Task while it runs. Washing up is otherwise Free — see Three Moments. Starting the rep is what converts it.
2Crossing a threshold2 sec · many times a day
The doorway is the trigger. Step through and put attention on the room as it is before deciding anything about it. Two seconds, then carry on.
Failure signal: you have already decided what the room means before you finished entering it.
3Five minutes of ground5 min · daily
Walking, or standing still in the yard. One outward channel — what you see, or what you hear. Not both. When the narrative starts, “not relevant”, return to the channel.
Failure signal: you are rating how well the five minutes went. That is the audit, running inside the drill.

This is a rep, so it is Task while it runs. A walk is otherwise Free, and drifting on it is not a lapse. The rep is a deliberate five minutes inside that.
4A live hour with your daughters1 hour · 3× weekly
Phone away. No steering — no working out how to make it a better experience, no moving the conversation somewhere more valuable. Whatever it is, that is what it is.
Failure signal: the improving thought. This is the rung the whole protocol is for, and it sits at home because home is where you already know the ground.
Out of the house
5One task to its line20 min · daily
A single work task with a done-when set before you start. No quality judgement while working — the only permitted question at the end is whether the done-when is met.
Failure signal: one more pass after the line is met. That is the polish reflex, and it is deliberation returning through the quality door.
6A teaching block80 min · 2× weekly
The structure is imposed and not yours to redesign, which makes it the highest-quality reps available to you. Run it with attention on the room, not on your delivery. Passing time is the review window.
Failure signal: you are teaching and simultaneously editing the lesson. Note it; edit after.
7One unaudited conversation1 per day
A conversation you would normally assess as it happens. No tally, no rating, nothing counted. Attention entirely on them. Review after, once, inside the window — or not at all.
Failure signal: you know, mid-conversation, how it is going. Noticing that is fine; acting on it is the miss.
8Krav, at full paceas trained
The ceiling rung, and the only one that enforces itself. You cannot monitor and spar. Adrenaline removes the option, which is why this is the one place the skill is already implicit.
Failure signal: none available in the moment — that is the point. Use it as the reference for what the other seven are aiming at.
Rungs 6 and 7 are deliberately late. A new building, new colleagues and new students make every ordinary thing unfamiliar, and unfamiliar ground recruits the self-monitoring this protocol exists to remove. Get to know the place first; the reps will be worth more when you do.
The One Thing
Freeze the system for eight weeks and get to rung 4.
Everything else here is support. If only one thing happens, it is the live hour with no steering — not instrumented, not scored, and the reason the exit is being built at all. It is rung 4 rather than rung 8 because it is home ground, and because it should not wait for October.
Failure Modes
Building as practice — the most convincing one. A new card produces a visible change tonight; a rep produces nothing observable for weeks. The feedback favours the wrong arm.
Unbounded Over — the evaluation window with no closing edge. It does not stay in the past.
Practising on unlearnable ground — grinding reps where no feedback exists produces confidence and nothing else.
Reading more about it — understanding this is orientation work, and orientation is already your strong side. A reading project is a new loop; it waits for Sunday like anything else.
Turning the drill into a checklist you run mid-moment — that is the auditor with a clipboard, which is the exact thing being removed. The Drill tab is review-only. Never during.
Evidence
A Strong. Kahneman & Klein (2009) on the conditions for intuitive expertise — an adversarial collaboration, which is why it carries weight. Automaticity as instance retrieval rather than faster processing (Logan 1988); Fitts & Posner's three stages.

B Good, with variance. Monitoring degrading skilled execution (Beilock & Carr); Masters' reinvestment theory — explicitly learned rules get consciously reinvested under pressure, implicitly acquired skill is more robust. Replicated, effect sizes vary by task and population.

C Extrapolated. March's exploration/exploitation drift is an organisational model; applying it to one person is a borrowed shape, not a tested finding. It fits your record well, which is not the same as being established.

D Arbitrary. The eight-week span and every dose on the ladder. No evidence sets those numbers. They are fixed in advance so they cannot be renegotiated in week three, and that is their only justification.

Deliberately excluded: the popular 10,000-hours framing — the Macnamara meta-analysis found deliberate practice explains far less variance than the popular version claims, and the number itself was never a finding.
