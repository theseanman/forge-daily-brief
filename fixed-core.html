🧭🌴Orientation — The Fixed Core
tap to add your orientation image
When looking bad collides with a hard line, the hard line wins. Every time. No deliberation.
Values
1. My family thrives, and I do whatever is in my power to make that happen.
2. I do not lose or fail due to circumstances within my control.
3. The world is often harsh, and I will meet harshness with mental and physical toughness.
4. When others outperform me, I close the gap through cunning, preparation, and strategy. I never give up and I never accept defeat.
Mission
Primary — family thriving. Secondary — life lived on my own terms. When they collide, primary wins.
Hard Lines
1. I do not quit in competition with another person.
2. I do not abandon my family in any way.
3. I do not put my own needs above my family's.
↳ tap: the maintenance exception
Operational maintenance — sleep, training, health, thinking time, presence — is not "needs above family's." It is what makes serving them possible at all. Optional indulgence — hobbies or comfort competing with family time — is "needs above." A depleted father cannot deliver on Value 1.
4. I do not submit or perform half-assed work.
Force
Use force only to protect family — never to punish or prove. Peers' opinions are data, not authority.

Pre-Loaded Responses
When a task hits its done-when — it's finished. Close it and move to the next. No polishing past the line.
When I reach for a small task while the important one is open — name it "not relevant" and go back to the important one.
When a new income idea excites me — write it down; it waits 24 hours. A Sunday commitment isn't renegotiated by daytime doubt.
When something I tried fails in front of people — it's data, not quitting. The hard line is about not starting, not about failing.
⚠ Failure modes — tap
Dogma drift — the core goes closed to review; the annual review has to be real, not ceremonial.
Periphery invasion — a bad day triggers a core review; Sunday sorts it, three Sundays queues it for the annual.
Rehearsal decay — the daily recall gets skipped into nothing; attach it to an existing habit.
False pre-decision — a value claimed but never tested under pressure; test it at small scale first.
Value-collision paralysis — two values point opposite and you freeze; pre-decide the collision at Sunday review.
Fake toughness — Value 3 worn as a costume in every room; toughness is a capacity held ready, not a personality.
🧭🌴Pre-Decided Responses
Family
Does this serve my #1 priority of ensuring that my family is thriving? If in doubt, the family-serving choice wins.
Income
Does this build toward the exit? New ideas wait 24 hours, then go to Sunday — not daytime doubt. Trying and failing isn’t quitting — quitting is not starting. A public mistake is information, not a verdict.
Teaching
Do the work well and go home to my family and my build. Colleagues’ and admin’s opinions are data, not authority; pushback doesn’t trigger a value review.
Health
Zone 2, lifting, Krav, sleep are non-negotiable maintenance. A real family need wins the day; chronic displacement of training is a Sunday fix, not a daily surrender.
Financial
Does this move toward the exit? Lifestyle inflation is the enemy. 24-hour rule on any appealing purchase.
Physical threat
Family out first; engage only if extraction requires it. Force to protect — never to punish or prove.
