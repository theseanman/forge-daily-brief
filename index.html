🧠🌴Mental Model — Margin of Safety
Where in today's plan is there no margin? What happens when that piece runs long?
What it is & how it applies▾
Benjamin Graham
Build in more slack than your estimate requires, because your estimate is wrong in ways you can't specify. The size of the margin should scale with the cost of being wrong.
In practice
Plan the day at seventy percent capacity. The other thirty is where reality lives.
Don't quit the salary at the point where the numbers just barely work.
If a lesson needs the full block with no slack, it will fail the first time the projector doesn't work.
