TEMPO

⏸️ PAUSE BEFORE MOVING, DECIDING OR ACTING
When I catch myself drawing breath to speak — let it out without words, shoulders down, then speak.
Four places it applies: speaking · reaching · deciding · getting up and moving. Each is counted separately at the debrief, so you can see where it lands and where it does not.
Three today. Not all day. Raise it when three is boring.
🐢 SLOW MOVEMENTS & SPEECH
Deliberate, slower pace in all physical and verbal communication. Move 20% slower than your impulse — reach, turn, walk slower.
Cue: all day
🤝 I’M FINE EITHER WAY
Before the first tense interaction, say it silently and mean it. You would like it to go well; you do not need it to.
Cue: before tension

🧘 NOT RELEVANT
When the optimizing narrative starts — how to improve this, what it should become — name it and take away its authority: not relevant to being present with my family. It can stay. It does not get a vote.
Scope: optimization thoughts, plus two that hide in free time — the LOOP (circling why am I like this) and the REPLAY (rehearsing a conversation already over). Not a general silencer: some internal signal is load-bearing, and emotion is not a thought to be dismissed. Two seconds, then back to one sense channel.
Cue: the moment it starts
