The idea
Surfaces and gapsDOCTRINE
Cheng is the expected force: it meets the opponent’s surfaces — their strengths — and holds them. Ch’i is the unexpected force that goes through the gaps. Sun Tzu named the pair; Boyd built it into Patterns of Conflict, and maneuver-warfare writers turned it into “avoid surfaces, exploit gaps.”
Every strength is paid for with something. The gap is usually whatever the opponent spent to build their surface. The left-wing lock took away space; teams that beat it stopped fighting for space and won on time — countering at speed before the lock could set.
Boyd sorted conflict into three levels — physical, mental and moral — and ranked the moral level highest. The sixteen domains below sit inside those three.
Who is across from you? With a genuine counterparty — a seller, a dispute, a competitor — use all of it. With your daughters, your students, your family, the other person is not an enemy: use the domains to pick the ground where a good outcome for both of you becomes reachable, never to beat them. Your Patterns of Conflict page draws the same line. “Where to look for their weakness” is for counterparties only.

Seen in the world
Hockey, late 1990s. The left-wing lock and the trap took away neutral-zone space. Counter-attacking teams moved the puck through before the structure set. Space → time. VERIFIED, IN OUTLINE
Korea, F-86 vs MiG-15. The MiG climbed and turned better; the F-86’s hydraulic controls let it change manoeuvres faster. Boyd’s seed for tempo. DEBATED
Kinshasa, 1974. Ali let Foreman punch himself out on the ropes. Power → energy.
Thermopylae. A narrow pass made Persian numbers irrelevant. Resources → space.
Midway. Codebreakers told the weaker fleet where and when. Strength → information.
Inchon. MacArthur landed where the tides made a landing look impossible. Defence → expectation.
Sherman’s march. Threatening two cities at once, so the defender guarded both and arrived at neither in strength. Liddell Hart’s “horns of a dilemma.” Attention.
Fabius vs Hannibal. Refused the pitched battles Hannibal was best at; time wore down an army far from home. Not engaging + patience.
Cannae. Hannibal let Rome’s aggressive centre push forward into its own encirclement. Their will became their weakness.
Moneyball. Oakland bought on-base percentage while the market paid for batting average. Resources → information.
Drone war. A cheap drone forces a missile worth far more. The defender wins the exchange and loses the cost war.
From general knowledge, not looked up for this page. The pattern drawn from them is reasoning, not research.
